1

Telling Time (Jam)

🕐
Telling time adalah cara menyebutkan waktu dalam bahasa Inggris menggunakan jam & menit.
  • Pertanyaan waktu: 'What time is it?'
  • Jam tepat: 'It's seven o'clock' (jam 7 tepat).
  • Lewat: 'It's seven fifteen' (jam 7 lewat 15) atau 'quarter past seven'.
  • Kurang: 'It's seven forty-five' atau 'quarter to eight' (jam 8 kurang 15).
  • a.m. = pagi/siang (00:00-11:59). p.m. = sore/malam (12:00-23:59).
Contoh07:30 = It's seven thirty. 14:00 = It's two p.m. 06:15 = It's quarter past six.
2

Daily Activities

📅
Daily activities adalah kegiatan sehari-hari yang dilakukan secara rutin.
  • Morning (pagi): wake up (bangun), take a bath (mandi), have breakfast (sarapan), go to school (pergi sekolah).
  • Afternoon (sore): go home (pulang), do homework (PR), play (bermain).
  • Evening (malam): have dinner (makan malam), study (belajar), sleep (tidur).
  • Kalimat rutin pakai s untuk subjek he/she/it: He wakes up at five.
  • Untuk I/you/we/they tanpa s: I wake up at five.
ContohI take a bath at 5 a.m. She goes to school at 6 a.m. They play football in the afternoon.
3

Taste (Rasa)

👅
Taste adalah kata sifat untuk rasa makanan & minuman.
  • Sweet = manis (gula, madu).
  • Sour = asam (jeruk, lemon).
  • Salty = asin (garam).
  • Bitter = pahit (kopi).
  • Spicy = pedas (cabe).
  • Kalimat: 'The mango is sweet.' / 'Is the soup salty?'
ContohThis candy is sweet. The lemon is sour. The coffee is bitter.
4

Size & Shape

📐
Size adalah ukuran benda, shape adalah bentuk benda.
  • Size: big (besar), small (kecil), long (panjang), short (pendek), tall (tinggi).
  • Shape: circle (lingkaran), square (persegi), triangle (segitiga), rectangle (persegi panjang).
  • Kalimat: 'The table is big.' / 'It is a circle.'
  • Tanya ukuran: 'How big is it?' Tanya bentuk: 'What shape is it?'
  • Kombinasi: 'It is a small square box.'
ContohBall = small circle. Door = tall rectangle. Book = rectangular.
5

Toys (Mainan)

🧸
Toys adalah benda untuk bermain, sering digunakan anak-anak.
  • Contoh toys: ball, doll, car, robot, kite, teddy bear, puzzle, blocks.
  • Kalimat: 'I have a doll.' / 'She has a robot.'
  • Tanya: 'Do you have a kite?' / 'What toys do you like?'
  • Jawab: 'Yes, I do.' / 'No, I don't.' / 'I like cars.'
  • Hobi bermain: 'My hobby is playing puzzles.'
ContohI have a teddy bear. He has a toy car. We play with kites.
